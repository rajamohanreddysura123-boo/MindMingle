Privacy Policy • Last Updated: August 13, 2026

About MindMingle: MindMingle is a social discovery, networking, and conversation platform designed for individuals to connect, talk about diverse topics, learn new things, share interests, and explore meaningful social connections with like-minded people around the world (not limited to dating).

Welcome to MindMingle ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications (Android & iOS) and related services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Setup & Authentication: When you register, we collect your phone number (for OTP verification), email address, and authentication credentials (including Google Sign-In data if chosen).
  • Profile Data: Your public profile details such as display name, age, bio, interests, learning goals, occupation, preferences ("looking for"), and profile photos uploaded by you.
  • User-Generated Content: Chat messages, text, media, voice notes, and communications exchanged with other users or support staff on MindMingle.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / APNs).
  • Log Data: App activity, crash reports, performance metrics, and feature interactions.

C. Payment & Subscription Information

  • If you subscribe to MindMingle+, payment transactions are processed securely through trusted third-party payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay or Google Play Billing). We store order IDs and subscription statuses but never store your raw credit card or banking credentials on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Facilitate Social Discovery & Connections: Enabling you to discover profiles, filter like-minded users by interests, hobbies, learning topics, and conversation goals.
  • To Provide Real-Time Communication: Delivering messages, chat notifications, and interaction feeds.
  • To Ensure Account Safety & Security: Authenticating logins, preventing spam, detecting policy violations, and banning fraudulent accounts.
  • To Improve Our Services: Analyzing usage trends and optimizing app performance across Android, iOS, and desktop targets.
  • To Process Transactions: Verifying subscription plans and maintaining billing histories.
  • To Deliver Advertisements (if applicable): Displaying non-intrusive ad banners or interstitial slots via Google AdMob (unless upgraded to an ad-free MindMingle+ subscription).

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share your information only under the following circumstances:

  • Public Profile Visibility: Your display name, photos, age, and interest tags are visible to other MindMingle users to facilitate social discovery.
  • Service Providers: We share necessary data with trusted cloud service providers (e.g., Google Firebase for database, authentication, storage, and push notifications; Razorpay for payments; Google AdMob for ads).
  • Legal Compliance & Safety: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of MindMingle, its users, or the public.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You have full control over your data:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can delete your account permanently directly within the MindMingle app settings (via "Delete Account"). This removes your profile, photos, matches, chat history, and tokens from our systems.
  • Photo Removal: You can edit or delete uploaded photos at any time in your profile editor.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and secure access controls (Firebase Cloud Firestore security rules & Cloud Functions) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Children's Privacy & Child Safety Standards

Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE/CSAM): MindMingle maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding any content or behavior related to Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

  • Strict Age Restriction: MindMingle is strictly intended for users aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly permit minors to create accounts or use our services.
  • CSAM Detection & Reporting: Any attempt to upload, transmit, or share CSAM/CSAE content will result in an immediate, permanent account ban, IP blocking, and reporting to legal authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and regional law enforcement.
  • In-App Reporting: Users can report suspicious activity or safety concerns directly in-app or via our designated safety point of contact at rajamohanreddysura006@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy or through in-app notifications.
